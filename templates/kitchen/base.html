WELCOME TO YOUR KITCHEN

A little help, a lot of joy.

Savor is your personal space for the food you love.

1. Fill your recipe box
Add your own recipes or explore the ones already here. Tap a heart to keep your favorites close.

2. Make a little plan
Open the meal planner and choose a recipe for any day. You can adjust servings for each meal.

3. Shop with ease
Add your pantry stock. Your shopping list combines uncooked meals and subtracts what you already have. Add checked purchases straight to your pantry.

4. Cook, record, repeat
Find recipes with the fewest missing ingredients, then use “I cooked this” to update stock. Cooking history keeps your meals and lets you undo a mistake.

This is a personal, single-kitchen app. Your recipes and plans are saved on this computer.
